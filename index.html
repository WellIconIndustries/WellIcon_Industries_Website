Well Icon India — Clothing Mankind Since 1876

Founded in 1876, Well Icon pioneered innerwear, evolving and innovating not only the product, but also the way it has been marketed over the years. Well Icon is a leading manufacturer and marketer of comfort apparel sold in more than 140 countries around the world. The company is committed to quality, comfort, fashion, innovation and value. As Well Icon grows and sophistication, the simple commitment to serve its consumer’s need for comfort continues to be the brand’s hallmark.
